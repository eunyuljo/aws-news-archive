# Amazon DynamoDB Accelerator (DAX) is now available in additional Regions

**날짜:** 2026년 10월 30일
**서비스:** RDS
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/dax-additional-regions/

## 내용

Amazon DynamoDB Accelerator (DAX) is now available in two additional AWS Regions: Canada (Central) and Africa (Cape Town). DAX is a fully managed, highly available, in-memory cache for Amazon DynamoDB that delivers up to ten times performance improvement, from single-digit milliseconds to microseconds, even at millions of requests per second. 
With DAX, you can accelerate read-intensive and bursty workloads such as real-time bidding, gaming leaderboards, and retail product catalogs, while offloading read traffic from your DynamoDB tables. DAX is API-compatible with DynamoDB, so you can add microsecond-latency caching to your applications with minimal code changes. Customers can now keep cached data within the Region and meet low-latency requirements while benefiting from a fully managed service, including automated patching and failover. 
To learn more, see the Amazon DynamoDB Accelerator page. For the full list of Regions where DAX is available, see the AWS Regional Services page.
