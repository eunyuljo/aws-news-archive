# Structured outputs for Claude models available on Amazon Bedrock

**날짜:** 2026년 10월 21일
**서비스:** RDS
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/claude-structured-outputs-bedrock/

## 내용

Amazon Bedrock now supports structured outputs for all Anthropic Claude models across both the bedrock-runtime and the bedrock-mantle endpoint. Whether you call Claude through boto3 and the bedrock-runtime APIs or through the Messages API and the bedrock-mantle endpoint, you can now receive schema-compliant JSON responses that conform to a defined structure, without custom parsing, retry logic, or post-processing. 
With structured outputs for Claude on Amazon Bedrock, you can automate reliable, schema-compliant tasks across industries. Examples include extracting every required field from financial earnings reports and compliance documents, parsing clinical notes into Electronic Health Records (EHR), and enriching product catalogs with consistent, validated results. You can also analyze contracts for key terms, parties, and obligations, or power intelligent support ticket routing where intents, sentiments, and entities always match your application's data model. 
To learn more, see the Amazon Bedrock documentation&nbsp;. Structured outputs is available where claude models are available. For more information check the&nbsp;regional availability details page.
