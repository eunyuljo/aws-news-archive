# AWS Direct Connect announces 100G expansion in Manchester, England

**날짜:** 2026년 10월 22일
**서비스:** Connect
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/aws-direct-connect-100g-manchester/

## 내용

Today, AWS announced the expansion of 10 Gbps and 100 Gbps dedicated connections with MACsec encryption capabilities at the existing AWS Direct Connect location in the Equinix MA3 data center near Manchester, England. You can now establish private, direct network access to all public AWS Regions (except those in China), AWS GovCloud Regions, and AWS Local Zones from this location. This is the third AWS Direct Connect location in England to provide 100 Gbps connections with MACsec encryption capabilities. 
The Direct Connect service enables you to establish a private, physical network connection between AWS and your data center, office, or colocation environment. These private connections can provide a more consistent network experience than those made over the public internet. 
For more information on the over 150 Direct Connect locations worldwide, visit the locations section of the Direct Connect product detail pages. Or, visit our getting started page to learn more about how to purchase and deploy Direct Connect.
