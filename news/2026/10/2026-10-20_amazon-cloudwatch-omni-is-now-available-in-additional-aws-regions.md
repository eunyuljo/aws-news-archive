# Amazon CloudWatch Omni is now available in additional AWS Regions

**날짜:** 2026년 10월 20일
**서비스:** CloudWatch
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/amazon-cloudwatch-omni-8-regions/

## 내용

AWS announces that Amazon CloudWatch Omni is now available in eight additional AWS Regions. CloudWatch Omni is an AI-powered observability experience, organized around your teams and the applications they run, that helps you observe and troubleshoot your applications and agents in one place. It combines the interoperability of OpenTelemetry with the scale and reliability of CloudWatch, and meets you where you work — a standalone web experience with single sign-on (SSO), or a local IDE extension.
With CloudWatch Omni, You create spaces in your central accounts to see telemetry across your AWS accounts, Regions, and other clouds, including Azure and GCP. Omni discovers services, maps dependencies, and surfaces golden metrics automatically and you can interact with telemetry through chat, point-and-click, or your own tool via Agent Toolkit for AWS to find signals, build dynamic views, and reach root cause powered by AWS DevOps Agent. A dedicated agent observability experience adds an evaluation-driven workflow for frameworks including LangGraph, CrewAI, OpenAI Agents SDK, Vercel AI SDK, and Strands.
With this expansion, CloudWatch Omni is now available in Canada (Central), Asia Pacific (Mumbai), Asia Pacific (Singapore), Asia Pacific (Sydney), Asia Pacific (Tokyo), Europe (Frankfurt), Europe (London), and South America (São Paulo), in addition to US East (N. Virginia), US West (Oregon), and Europe (Ireland).
To get started, create your Omni space from the CloudWatch console, configure SSO, and sign in. Agent developers can install the free CloudWatch Omni extension for VS Code, Cursor, and Kiro. To learn more, see the Amazon CloudWatch Omni product page and documentation. For pricing, see the CloudWatch Omni pricing page.
