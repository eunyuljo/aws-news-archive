# SageMaker MLflow Apps now support MLflow 3.16

**날짜:** 2026년 10월 28일
**서비스:** SageMaker
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/mflow-version-3-16/

## 내용

Amazon SageMaker AI MLflow Apps now support MLflow 3.16, bringing a redesigned tracing experience and faster experiment tracking to your generative AI and machine learning workflows. 
MLflow 3.16 improves debugging and analysis of generative AI applications and agents. The redesigned trace explorer is now the default, with adjustable row height, reorderable columns, custom columns from any trace tag or metadata field. This release also accelerates metric charts for experiments with large metric histories. 
New MLflow Apps are created on MLflow 3.16 by default. Existing MLflow Apps will be upgraded to MLflow 3.16 during an upcoming maintenance window, with no action required. 
MLflow 3.16 is available in all AWS Regions where MLflow Apps are available. To learn more, visit the SageMaker AI MLflow developer guide.
