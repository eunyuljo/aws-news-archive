# Amazon Connect Customer now lets you view metrics by customer lifetime value and repeat-caller status

**날짜:** 2026년 10월 27일
**서비스:** Connect
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/connect-customer-boma-segment/

## 내용

Now you can view your metrics across customer segments: customer lifetime value (LTV) and repeat callers. See how any metric differs for repeat callers versus the rest of your contacts, or how results hold up among your highest-LTV customers; so you can spot the groups trending the wrong way before it shows up in the aggregate. Where a segment is underperforming, you can act. Route high-LTV customers to your most effective people, adjust the knowledge bases and self-service flows that repeat callers are falling back from, or refine agent guidance for segments where satisfaction is slipping. The segment tells you where to look; the metrics tell you what to fix.
