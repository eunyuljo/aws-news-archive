# AWS End User Messaging adds a guided wizard for RCS registration videos

**날짜:** 2026년 10월 17일
**서비스:** General
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/aws-end-user-messaging-adds-guided-wizard-rcs-registration-videos

## 내용

Today, AWS End User Messaging adds a wizard that orchestrates the entire video capture for your RCS agent registration. RCS registrations in multiple countries, including the United States, require a screen recording that demonstrates your messaging experience. To record it, you have to send a specific sequence of messages to a test device, and until now you scripted those messages yourself, which easily introduced errors and made a mismatched or incomplete video one of the hardest parts of registering for RCS.
 
 
 The wizard now drives each step of the capture for you, sending the right messages in the right order, so you record a compliant video the first time and get your agent approved sooner. The wizard is on all RCS registration forms that require a video submission.
 
 
 To learn more and get started, see the AWS End User Messaging User Guide.
