# AWS Builder Center now available as mobile app on iOS and Android

**날짜:** 2026년 09월 18일
**서비스:** Connect
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/09/aws-builder-center-now-available-as-mobile-app/

## 내용

AWS Builder Center is a platform that brings together the global builder community, offering articles, workshops, sandbox environments, events, and learning resources in one place. Previously limited to desktop and web access, the experience is now available as a mobile app on iOS and Android. Builders worldwide can stay connected and engaged with the community from anywhere, using their AWS Builder ID to remain signed in across sessions. 
The AWS Builder Center mobile app enables builders to browse trending articles, access 600+ AWS Skill Builder courses, and manage hands-on workshops with free sandbox environments directly from their mobile devices. Builders can follow AWS Heroes, Community Builders, and User Group Leaders, check Builder Loft event calendars on the go, and receive push notifications for subscribed topics and communities. The app also supports the Wishlist feature, allowing builders to submit and manage product feedback directly to AWS teams. 
The AWS Builder Center mobile app is available worldwide on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. 
To learn more about the AWS Builder Center mobile app, visit the AWS Builder Center mobile page.
