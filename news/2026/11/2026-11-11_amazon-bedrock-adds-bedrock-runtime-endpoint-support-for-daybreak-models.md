# Amazon Bedrock adds bedrock-runtime endpoint support for Daybreak models

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 11일
**서비스:** Bedrock
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/openai-gpt-daybreak-on-amazon-bedrock-runtime/

## 내용

Eligible customers can now access OpenAI’s specialized cybersecurity models Daybreak Red: GPT-5.6 Cyber and Daybreak Blue: GPT-5.6 Sol through the bedrock-runtime endpoint on Amazon Bedrock.  Daybreak Blue is the starting point for most security teams across defensive workflows, including vulnerability discovery, detection engineering, and incident response. Daybreak Red is designed for advanced, authorized tasks such as vulnerability research, exploit reproduction, and mitigation development. For these tasks, a lower refusal threshold, matched by stronger identity verification, monitoring, and access controls, improves the speed and depth of an investigation. Your inference data is not used for model training, and neither model requires you to opt in to sharing your data with OpenAI.  On the bedrock-runtime endpoint, the models are available through US and Global cross-Region inference (CRIS) profiles. Access to the models requires enrollment in Daybreak access from OpenAI. To enroll, contact OpenAI or reach out to your AWS account team for guidance on eligibility. Once approved, work with your account team to request access on AWS. For regional availability through the bedrock-mantle endpoint and more information, view the documentation.
