# Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus supports 2B active metrics and 300K rules per workspace

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 21일
**서비스:** General
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/amp-supports-2b-metrics-300k-rules/

## 내용

Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus now supports up to 2 billion active metric time series and up to 300,000 total recording and alerting rules per workspace. Customers can also create multiple workspaces per account. This lets them store and analyze billions of Prometheus metrics across their organization. 
Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus is a fully managed, Prometheus-compatible monitoring service. It helps you monitor and alert on operational metrics at scale. It scales ingestion and storage automatically for high-cardinality workloads across containerized, serverless and hybrid environments.  
To get started, create an Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus workspace. To raise your active series or rules limits, file a service limit increase request in AWS Support Center or AWS Service Quotas. To learn more, see the Amazon Managed Service for Prometheus user guide and the product page.
