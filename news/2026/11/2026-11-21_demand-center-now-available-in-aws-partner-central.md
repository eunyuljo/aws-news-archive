# Demand Center now available in AWS Partner Central

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 21일
**서비스:** Connect
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/demand-center-partner-central/

## 내용

AWS Partners can now plan, launch, fund, and measure demand generation campaigns in one AI-powered experience. Demand Center in AWS Partner Central succeeds Marketing Central and brings together Marketing Central and MDF capabilities, letting you work in Partner Central, in your CRM, or in your AI agents.
Create hero and distribution assets in multiple languages in minutes with AI campaign creation. Generate content from reference materials, a URL, or a description, and launch it across email, social, and banner ads. Upload leads to get them enriched with propensity signals and recommended next actions. Choose from ready-to-run Partner Ready Campaign kits or connect with AWS-vetted agencies through Agency Connect.
Generate case studies with AI from ACE closed-won opportunities, and tailor campaign content to the leads included in each campaign. Connect your CRM or marketing automation tool agent-to-agent, or use Demand Center inside agents such as AWS Quick through the Partner Central MCP server. Track campaign-to-revenue performance in Partner Analytics.
To get started, sign in to AWS Partner Central and select Demand Center.
