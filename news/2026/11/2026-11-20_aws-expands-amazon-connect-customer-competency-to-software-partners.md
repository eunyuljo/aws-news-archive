# AWS expands Amazon Connect Customer Competency to Software Partners

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 20일
**서비스:** Connect
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/connect-competency-software-partners/

## 내용

AWS announces the expansion of the Amazon Connect Customer Competency to Software Partners. The AWS Specialization now recognizes both Services and Software Partners with technical depth and proven success on Amazon Connect Customer—from migrating legacy contact centers and operationalizing AI at scale, to building new solutions on top of Amazon Connect Customer.
Modern customers want smooth, tailored interactions wherever they engage, but legacy customer experience systems weren't designed to provide them. They rely on queues, manual routing, and handle-time metrics as control mechanisms, with AI added as a separate layer rather than built in from the start.
The Competency adds three new categories for Software Partners: Customer Experience – Solution Extensions, for solutions that integrate with Amazon Connect Customer across the customer experience lifecycle; Connect A2A Collaboration – Agent Certification, for third-party AI agents certified to work alongside Amazon Connect Customer native AI agents during live customer contacts; and Connect Customer – Powered Partner Platform, for Partner-owned solutions that include Amazon Connect Customer as a core component. These join the two existing categories for Services Partners: Contact Center Transformation and AI-Powered Customer Experience.
As the first AWS Competency directly aligned to an AWS service, it gives customers confidence they're working with Partners validated by AWS experts to deliver AI-native customer experiences.
AWS Partners looking to get their solutions validated through the AWS Specialization Program must be at Advanced or Premier tier (Services Partners) or have an approved Foundational Technical Review (Software Partners) prior to applying. To learn more and discover validated Partners, visit the Amazon Connect Customer Competency page or the APN blog.
