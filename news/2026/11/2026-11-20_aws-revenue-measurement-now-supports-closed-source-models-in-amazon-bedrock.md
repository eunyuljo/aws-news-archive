# AWS Revenue Measurement now supports closed-source models in Amazon Bedrock

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 20일
**서비스:** Bedrock
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/revenue-measurement-bedrock/

## 내용

AWS Revenue Measurement (previously Partner Revenue Measurement) provides measurement of AWS consumption driven by Partner solutions. Today, AWS announces expanded Amazon Bedrock support in AWS Revenue Measurement. Partners can now gain attributed revenue visibility for their solutions' use of closed-source models in Amazon Bedrock, giving them a more complete view of the revenue their solutions drive. 
Until now, only Amazon models (such as Amazon Nova) and open-weight models in Amazon Bedrock were included in AWS Revenue Measurement. With this update, attributed revenue for Amazon Bedrock reflects the total Bedrock charges, including closed-source model usage. Partners who already use Resource Tagging, User Agent string, Identity Tagging, or SaaS Account Tagging for Amazon Bedrock automatically benefit from the expanded coverage, with no additional implementation needed. 
AWS Revenue Measurement is generally available in all commercial regions. To learn more, review the Amazon Bedrock best practices in the AWS Revenue Measurement onboarding guide.
