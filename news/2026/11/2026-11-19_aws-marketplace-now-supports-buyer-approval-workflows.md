# AWS Marketplace now supports buyer approval workflows

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 19일
**서비스:** General
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/marketplace-buyer-approvals/

## 내용

Today, AWS Marketplace announces the general availability of buyer approvals. This lets you decide which AWS Marketplace purchases need sign-off, who approves them, and where approval happens. You can define buying policies for the conditions that matter to you, such as purchase amount, product type, pricing, and whether an offer is public or private. Additionally, you can choose to route each purchase request to the right place: the AWS Marketplace console, a chain of approvers by email, or your procurement system in Coupa or SAP Ariba.
When you route approvals by email, procurement, finance, legal, and cloud governance teams can approve requests without needing to sign into the AWS console. They see the product, price, terms, and can approve right from their email. For example, you can route large software commitments to Coupa or SAP Ariba for review, send mid-size purchases to multiple approvers by email, and let low-cost or pay-as-you-go purchases go through without approval. When a purchase is routed to your procurement system, it follows your existing approval flows and gets a dedicated purchase order for cleaner spend tracking and reconciliation.
With AWS Marketplace, customers can already choose which subset of available products their teams can buy, track each purchase with purchase orders, and require their employees to enter a purchase order when buying. Now, with buyer approvals, you can also control how each purchase is approved before making a financial commitment.
Customers can now create buying policies and approvals in all AWS Marketplace commercial Regions. To get started, see the AWS Marketplace Buyer Guide.
