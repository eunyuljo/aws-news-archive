# Amazon Connect launches Resolutions, an agentic solution for high-volume operational work

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 20일
**서비스:** RDS
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/amazon-connect-resolutions/

## 내용

Amazon Connect Customer announces the general availability of Resolutions, an agentic operations solution that resolves high-volume work end-to-end, including refund requests, transaction disputes, fraud investigations, and insurance claims. Resolutions helps businesses reduce resolution times and cost to serve by combining the reasoning of AI agents with the reliability of deterministic workflows. Every request follows business policy, gets the oversight it requires, and ends in a verifiable outcome. 
Managers describe a process in natural language or upload standard operating procedures (SOPs), and Resolutions generates a Work plan in minutes. As it builds the Work plan, Resolutions asks clarifying questions that surface exceptions, set decision rules, and define approval thresholds, closing the gaps an SOP leaves open. Resolutions then opens a Resolutions Case for each incoming request and applies the right Work plan. AI agents gather information, apply policy, act across your business applications, and ask customers for missing details to drive every Case to completion. Each Step can run on prebuilt AI agents, AI agents configured in Amazon Connect Customer, or your own, while human specialists handle approvals and exceptions with the context they need to act. 
For example, a bank resolving a transaction dispute can have AI agents pull the transaction history, check the claim against chargeback rules, email the cardholder for a missing receipt, and issue a provisional credit, so a specialist reviews only the disputes that need judgment. Resolutions verifies each outcome and records every action and decision, giving operations leaders an auditable, real-time view of progress, workload, and service level risk. 
To learn more, visit our documentation, webpage, and pricing page.
