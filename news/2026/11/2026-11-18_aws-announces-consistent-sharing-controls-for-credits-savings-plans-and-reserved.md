# AWS announces consistent sharing controls for credits, Savings Plans, and Reserved Instances

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 18일
**서비스:** Config
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/aws-benefits-sharing-capabilities/

## 내용

AWS Billing and Cost Management now gives customers symmetric, granular control over how credits, Reserved Instances, and Savings Plans are shared across their organization. Organizations with grant funding, regulatory obligations, or multi-entity structures can now automate benefit allocation that previously required manual chargeback reconciliation. With these new capabilities, customers can set default sharing preferences that automatically apply to all cost-saving benefits, and override those defaults for individual benefits when business requirements demand it. 
Customers configure Credit Group Sharing through the Billing Preferences page to set a single default that every new credit inherits automatically, ending the cycle of per-credit manual configuration. For Reserved Instances and Savings Plans, customers configure sharing preferences at the individual commitment level through the Savings Plans or Reserved Instances inventory pages, whether purchased at a linked account or at the payer level. All sharing configurations use Cost Categories, the same grouping construct customers already use in Cost Explorer, AWS Budgets, and the Cost and Usage Report. 
These capabilities are available starting November 17, 2026, in all AWS Regions through the AWS Billing and Cost Management Console and public APIs. There is no additional charge. To learn more, visit the for documentations for Credit Group Sharing&nbsp;and Savings Plan and Reserved Instances Level Sharing.
