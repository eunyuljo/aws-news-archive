# OpenAI GPT-6.2 Astra, and GPT-6.2 Sol are now generally available on Amazon Bedrock

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 18일
**서비스:** Bedrock
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/openai-gpt-6-2-astra-sol-amazon-bedrock/

## 내용

Today, AWS announces the general availability of OpenAI GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol on Amazon Bedrock. GPT-6.2 Astra brings frontier-level reasoning and judgment to your most ambitious work, including complex business problems, demanding research, and advanced coding and agentic workflows. Astra also includes an Ultrafast mode for latency-sensitive applications that need fast responses. GPT-6.2 Sol is designed for recurring coding, agentic, and professional workflows with clear outcomes, where speed and cost per task matter. 
Use GPT-6.2 Astra for challenging work that calls for expert judgment and a finished deliverable, such as reconciling competing inputs, investigating complex codebases, reasoning across text and images, and creating professional artifacts. Select ultrafast mode for Astra for interactive coding assistants, real-time agents, and customer-facing applications where latency shapes the customer experience. Use GPT-6.2 Sol for recurring, high-volume work with verifiable outcomes, such as implementing and debugging software, analyzing documents and data, and automating multistep workflows. Established AWS controls help you secure workloads, govern access, and audit model invocation activity.&nbsp; 
You can get started in the Amazon Bedrock console or programmatically through supported Amazon Bedrock APIs. For information about supported AWS Regions, endpoints, APIs, features, inference profiles and pricing, see the Amazon Bedrock documentation.
