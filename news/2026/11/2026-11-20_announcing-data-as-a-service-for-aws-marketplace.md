# Announcing Data as a Service for AWS Marketplace

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 20일
**서비스:** S3
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/aws-marketplace-data-as-a-service/

## 내용

Today, AWS Marketplace announces the general availability of Data as a Service, a new product type that lets customers buy third-party datasets and licensed content. Customers often have the models and compute they need, but buying third-party data means adding each provider as a new supplier, negotiating separate contracts, and waiting on procurement cycles. Customers can now find and subscribe to Data as a Service products from AWS Marketplace sellers, and use them to enrich artificial intelligence (AI) applications, analytics, and other workloads.
Customers purchase Data as a Service products through public or private offers, and charges appear on their existing AWS invoice. For example, an investment team evaluating private companies can connect its AI agents to private-market data through an API and to credit analysis through a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server.
Sellers set their own prices and license terms, using a contract, pay-as-you-go, or contract with consumption pricing model for each product. They can add a free trial to any of these models, or offer a product for free. They also define the onboarding and delivery experience, from setting up the customer's account to providing the data through an API, a web portal, a real-time feed, an Amazon Simple Storage Service (Amazon S3) bucket, or another approach.
To learn more, explore Data as a Service products on AWS Marketplace, the AWS Marketplace Buyer Guide, and the AWS Marketplace Seller Guide.
