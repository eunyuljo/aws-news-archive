# AWS Marketplace now supports buyer contact sharing at subscription

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 19일
**서비스:** General
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/marketplace-buyer-contact-sharing/

## 내용

AWS Marketplace now supports buyer contact sharing. When customers subscribe to a third-party product, they can share their contact information with the seller. This helps sellers onboard new customers, send deployment guides and setup instructions, provide technical support, and share product updates from the start of the subscription. 
Customers share their first name, last name, email address, company name, and country or region in the deployment contact information section of the AWS Marketplace procurement page. AWS Marketplace pre-fills this section from the customer's previous purchases, AWS Profile, or AWS account details where available, so customers don't need to enter it again for each subscription. Customers can complete or edit any field before they subscribe, and view what they shared on the agreement details page in the AWS Marketplace console. 
Sellers can view the customer's contact information on the agreement details page in AWS Partner Central, or retrieve it with the new GetAgreementContacts API to bring it into their onboarding and support workflows. Sellers of software as a service (SaaS) products can call the API to pre-fill their registration page, so customers confirm their details instead of entering them again. This capability is available in all commercial AWS Regions where AWS Marketplace is available. 
To learn more, see Sharing your contact information at subscription in the AWS Marketplace Buyer Guide and Receiving buyer contact information in the AWS Marketplace Seller Guide.
