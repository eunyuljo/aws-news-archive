# AWS Partner Central Agents now generate Value Realization Plans

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 20일
**서비스:** General
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/partner-central-agents-bvr-vrp

## 내용

Today, AWS announces that AWS Partner Central Agents can now generate Value Realization Plans (VRPs) for the Business Value Realization (BVR) motion in AWS Partner Central. The VRP is the plan of record for a BVR engagement. The agentic experience generates a VRP that includes the customer's business goals; plan for adoption, change management, and governance; and baseline key performance indicators (KPIs) for Partners to review and finalize. 
AWS Partner Central launched the BVR motion on June 16, 2026, to help Partners drive business outcomes for their customers by structuring AWS service adoption into defined stages, with funding tied to demonstrated value. With this launch, Partners in the BVR motion no longer start a VRP from a blank page. The agents recommend KPIs, ramp targets, and baseline measurements based on nomination data, opportunity context, and service benchmarks. They also flag gaps before submission, such as a lack of customer sponsorship, and identify next steps. Partners start from a complete draft and refine it with their customer. 
BVR is available in AWS Partner Central for consulting, system integrator, and managed services partners with Advanced or Premier tier status and a qualifying domain competency. This capability is available in the US East (N. Virginia) Region. 
To get started, partners sign in to AWS Partner Central and choose Funding benefits, Business Value Realization. After AWS approves a nomination, partners ask AWS Partner Central Agents, "Help me draft a Value Realization Plan for my BVR engagement." To learn more, see Business Value Realization.
