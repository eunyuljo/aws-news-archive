# Introducing Amazon EC2 T9g instances based on Graviton5

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 21일
**서비스:** EC2
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/ec2-t9g-instances-ga/

## 내용

AWS is announcing the general availability of new low-cost burstable Amazon EC2 T9g instances, which offer the best price performance among general-purpose&nbsp;EC2 instances. These instances are powered by AWS Graviton5 processors and built on the latest sixth generation AWS Nitro cards. T9g instances deliver up to 45% better price performance compared to previous generation T4g instances, and offer four sizes: nano, micro, small, and medium. These instances are designed for workloads requiring low to moderate CPU utilization, including data processing, login gateways, small databases, microservices, low-traffic websites, freemium services, staging and development, and short-duration compute tasks such as batch processing, event-driven functions, and CI/CD pipelines. 
T9g instances deliver up to 2.3x the compute performance, 1.5x higher network bandwidth, and 2.8x higher EBS bandwidth compared to T4g instances. They are built on the sixth-generation AWS Nitro System and feature the Nitro Isolation Engine, harnessing formal verification to provide mathematical assurance that customer workloads are isolated from each other and AWS operators, pioneering a new standard for mathematically proven cloud security.&nbsp; 
T9g instances use the same CPU credit system as T4g with Standard and Unlimited modes, so existing T customers can upgrade to T9g and immediately benefit from better price performance, enabling them to lower their TCO. For customers new to AWS or migrating from on-premises, T9g instances provide one of the most cost-effective entry points to run a variety of lightweight workloads. 
T9g.micro and T9g.small are available under the AWS Free Tier.&nbsp;T9g instances are available in US East (N. Virginia, Ohio), US West (N. California, Oregon), Canada (Central), South America (São Paulo), Europe (Frankfurt, Spain), and Asia Pacific (Malaysia, Singapore, Sydney, Tokyo) regions. T9g instances are available to purchase via Savings Plans, On-Demand instances, and Spot instances. To learn more, visit the Amazon EC2 T9g instances page.&nbsp;&nbsp;
