# Lead groups and channel recommendations now available in AWS Partner Central

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 21일
**서비스:** General
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/partner-central-lead-groups/

## 내용

AWS Partners can now organize enriched leads and plan outreach with lead groups and channel recommendations in AWS Partner Central. Enriched leads are automatically grouped by solution area and segmented into sales-ready, marketing-ready, and limited-potential groups, so you no longer have to segment leads by hand. Limited-potential leads help you avoid spending time and budget on leads unlikely to convert, lowering your cost of acquiring new customers. For each enriched lead, you also get guidance on whether phone or email may be more effective, along with AI-generated call scripts and email scripts to act on it.
Marketing-ready lead groups can be sent to Demand Center to create assets and launch targeted campaigns. Sales-ready leads can be exported to the Partner's CRM for direct outreach.
Lead groups and channel recommendations are available to ACE-eligible Partners on the Leads page in AWS Partner Central and through the AWS Partner Central Selling API.
To get started, sign in to AWS Partner Central and go to the Leads page.
