# AWS Partner Central Agents now guide higher-quality co-sell submissions and explain co-sell motions

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 20일
**서비스:** General
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/partner-central-agents-guide-high-quality-opp

## 내용

Today, AWS announces that AWS Partner Central Agents now guide AWS Partners to create and enrich opportunities for a high-quality submission, and explain the assigned co-sell motion of each opportunity and why AWS assigned it. Since the launch of Agentic co-selling on June 16, 2026, AWS Partner Central Agents score every opportunity with an Opportunity Quality Score and assign co-sell motions including AWS Field-engaged, Agent-engaged, or Partner-led. 
When partners create an opportunity, the agents now guide them to a high-quality submission by asking for the details the Opportunity Quality Score measures, such as the customer's business problem, dated next steps, the Partner Primary Need from AWS, and the AWS services the customer will adopt, based on the co-selling best-practice guidance. After submission, the agents explain the reason behind each opportunity's co-sell motion and why AWS assigned it. Where the partner can act, the agents recommend the next step: for Agent-engaged opportunities, how to raise the Opportunity Quality Score; for Partner-led opportunities where AWS co-sell support was not requested, how to progress using agents. As partners act on these recommendations, the score and motion recalculate, and Agent-engaged opportunities move closer to AWS Field-engaged. 
The new enhanced experience is available today to AWS Partners in the US East (N. Virginia) Region. Partners can also use the agentic experience in native AI tools like AWS Quick and Kiro, or through MCP in their own CRM. To get started, partners sign in to AWS Partner Central and open opportunity management. To learn more, see Agentic Co-sell engagement and the AWS Partner Central Agents MCP guide.
