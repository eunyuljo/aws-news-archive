# AWS Partner Central guides now available in the console and through AWS Partner Central Agents

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 21일
**서비스:** RDS
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/partner-central-guides-mcp/

## 내용

AWS announces AWS Partner Central guides with access through the &nbsp;AWS Partner Central console&nbsp; and the AWS Partner Central Agents Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. The guides give AWS Partners one place to search, read, and share content that AWS organizes to help partners grow and expand their business. The guides hold authoritative, partner-specific knowledge on programs, benefits, technical topics, marketing, funding, and more, which requires sign-in and permissions. Partners benefit from more accurate answers about programs, benefits, and requirements. 
Partners can browse by topic, search instantly across every guide, and see content based on their recent activity and AWS program updates. Partners can also ask AWS Partner Central Agents questions in their own words, such as "Which guides explain the requirements for the AWS Foundational Technical Review?" or "Show me everything in Migration Acceleration Program quick reference." Partner Central guides complement the public information about AWS Partner Central features in AWS documentation. 
AWS Partner Central guides are available to all AWS Partners in the AWS Partner Central console and through the AWS Partner Central Agents MCP server in the US East (N. Virginia) Region in English. Partners can bring these guides into tools such as Kiro, AWS Quick, CRM systems, and other AI clients. To learn more, visit &nbsp;Guides in the AWS Partner Central console&nbsp; or &nbsp;Getting started with the Partner Central Agents MCP server&nbsp;.
