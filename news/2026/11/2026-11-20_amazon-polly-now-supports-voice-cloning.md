# Amazon Polly now supports voice cloning

**날짜:** 2026년 11월 20일
**서비스:** Connect
**링크:** https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/amazon-polly-now-supports-voice-cloning/

## 내용

Today, we are excited to announce the general availability of voice cloning in Amazon Polly, as a gated release. Using a few minutes of recordings from a consenting speaker, you can now create a cloned voice that sounds like that speaker. The cloned voices run on the Amazon Polly Generative engine, which delivers similar naturalness and accuracy as the rest of the generative voices. Voice cloning launches in seven languages: English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, and Hindi. Each cloned voice speaks the language it was recorded in. The cloned voices are automatically encrypted and access-controlled: only you can access your cloned voices.
Amazon Polly is a managed service that turns text into natural-sounding speech. With voice cloning, you can give your brand one recognizable voice across IVR and contact centers, voice agents, mobile apps, advertising, and connected devices. You can also create multiple voices for content creation use cases such as audiobooks, e-learning, podcasts, videos, and game characters. Amazon Polly does not retain a copy of your voice recordings once the voice is created and doesn't use them to train Polly models.
Voice cloning is available in the following AWS Regions: US East (N. Virginia), US West (Oregon), Europe (Frankfurt), Europe (Zurich), Europe (London), Asia Pacific (Sydney), Asia Pacific (Seoul), Asia Pacific (Singapore), Asia Pacific (Tokyo), Canada (Central), and AWS GovCloud (US-West).
Voice cloning is already enabled for select Amazon Polly customers. To request access, contact your AWS account team or apply here. Building a cloned voice is free for Amazon Polly customers, and you pay for the speech you synthesize, on a pay-as-you-go basis. For more details, see the Amazon Polly documentation, and Amazon Polly Pricing page. For speech AI use cases, you can build with Amazon Polly, including voice cloning. Check out the Amazon Polly GitHub repo.
